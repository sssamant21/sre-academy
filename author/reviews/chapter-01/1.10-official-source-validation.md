## Validation Summary

No blocking factual issue was found in the Chapter 1.10 author draft.

The section is aligned with official Kubernetes documentation on these points:

- Deployment controllers reconcile desired replica count.
- Services select Pods by labels and route to ready endpoints through endpoint resources.
- Resource requests affect scheduling decisions.
- RBAC grants verbs on resources through Roles and bindings.
- PodDisruptionBudgets express voluntary disruption constraints.
- Running Pods do not necessarily mean user-facing service health.
- PVCs, PVs, StorageClasses, and reclaim policy require careful operational handling.
- ServiceAccount and RBAC design affects workload blast radius.
- Production readiness requires ownership, security, resource, reliability, observability, and recovery review.
