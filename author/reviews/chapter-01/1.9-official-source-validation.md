## Validation Summary

No blocking factual issue was found in the Chapter 1.9 author draft.

The draft is aligned with official Kubernetes documentation on these points:

- Kubernetes production environments require availability, scaling, security, access management, and workload resource planning.
- Workload resources manage Pods on behalf of users and support replacement-oriented operation.
- Deployments are a strong fit for replicated stateless services when rollout, readiness, and resource design are correct.
- StatefulSets provide stable identity for stateful workloads but do not themselves provide complete data reliability.
- Probes have distinct startup, readiness, and liveness semantics.
- Bad liveness probe design can worsen reliability.
- PodDisruptionBudgets help with voluntary disruptions but do not protect against all failure modes.
- Requests and limits are part of scheduling and runtime reliability.
- Services, NetworkPolicies, RBAC, and persistent storage each require explicit production design.
- HPA depends on metrics and available capacity and should not be treated as instant protection.
