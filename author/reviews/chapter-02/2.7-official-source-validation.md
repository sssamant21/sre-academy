## Notes

The section intentionally avoids claiming that Kubernetes storage snapshots are complete backups. It also avoids treating StorageClass names as
portable guarantees, because Kubernetes does not define the vendor-specific meaning of class names such as `fast`, `standard`, or `premium`.
