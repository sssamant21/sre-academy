## Notes

The section intentionally separates controller choice by production guarantee: Deployments for interchangeable stateless replicas, StatefulSets for
stable identity and storage, and DaemonSets for node-local coverage. It also treats StatefulSet PVC handling as a data-safety concern rather than a
routine cleanup detail.
