# Argo Workflows

The Argo Workflows handbook will document Kubernetes-native workflow automation for reliable jobs and pipelines.

## Planned chapters

- Workflow templates, steps, DAGs, and artifacts
- Scheduling, retries, timeouts, and failure handling
- Secrets, service accounts, and permissions
- Observability, archival, and cleanup
- Operational runbooks for stuck or failed workflows

## Starter reliability questions

1. Which workflows are critical to delivery or operations?
2. How are retries and failure states handled?
3. What resource limits protect the cluster from runaway jobs?
