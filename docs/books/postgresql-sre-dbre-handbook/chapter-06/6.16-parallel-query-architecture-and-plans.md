# 6.16 -- Parallel Query Architecture and Parallel Plans

**Status:** Complete -- Revised Final / Canonical Edition

Parallel plans use `Gather` or `Gather Merge` to coordinate a leader with worker processes. The planner may choose parallel scans, joins, aggregation, and append strategies where legal and beneficial. Planned workers may not all be available at runtime because cluster-wide worker limits and concurrent demand apply.

Parallelism is not free: it adds setup, CPU, memory, and coordination cost and can worsen fleet-wide contention. Review `Workers Planned` versus `Workers Launched`, per-worker distribution, CPU saturation, memory/spill, I/O bandwidth, and latency.

Functions/operations have parallel-safety classifications; unsafe operations can prevent parallel plans. Tune parallel settings only with workload-level capacity analysis.
