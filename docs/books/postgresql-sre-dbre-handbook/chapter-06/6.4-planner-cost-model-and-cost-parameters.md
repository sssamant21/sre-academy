# 6.4 -- Planner Cost Model and Cost Parameters

**Status:** Complete -- Revised Final / Canonical Edition

Planner costs are relative estimates used to compare candidate plans; they are not direct milliseconds. Important inputs include sequential and random page costs, CPU tuple/index/operator costs, parallel setup/tuple costs, effective cache assumptions, and estimated cardinalities.

Changing cost constants globally can alter many plans simultaneously. Do not tune them to repair one query. Establish representative workloads, storage characteristics, cache behavior, plan baselines, and regression tests first. Validate changes across OLTP, reporting, batch, and maintenance workloads and maintain rollback.

A wrong row estimate can dominate a perfectly reasonable cost model, so cardinality diagnosis precedes cost-constant tuning in most incidents.
