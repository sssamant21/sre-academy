# Answers: Chapters 16–20

Version: v1.2.0  
Status: Production Release  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-15

1. Labels distinguish verified product behavior from illustrative or privacy-protected experience and prevent unsupported generalization.
2. Provide baseline, workload/data context, query IDs, configuration, UTC window, evidence, change, validation and cost.
3. State environment and workload dependence, baseline/sample method, correctness, duration, cost and whether the result is illustrative rather than guaranteed.
4. Separation limits routine exposure; monitoring and expiry make exceptional access accountable and reduce standing privilege.
5. Owner role, grants, creation/change history, dependencies, rotation/expiry, operational owner and decommission plan.
6. Collect redacted errors/timestamps, verify username and public-key registration/rotation state, compare client configuration, never collect the private key.
7. The condition must be specific and observable; action bounded, reversible, idempotent where required, authorized and validated.
8. These controls prevent oscillation, repeated harm, silent mutation and loss of human control.
9. Review triggering metric freshness, thresholds, hysteresis/cooldown, workload classification, action history, cost and manual override.
10. Ownership assigns accountability, SLOs define outcomes, and escalation routes failures to empowered responders.
11. Purpose, owners, consumers, SLOs, dependencies, data classification, access, cost model, alerts, runbooks, recovery and lifecycle.
12. Service catalog, RACI/ownership matrix, SLO, on-call/escalation policy and incident-command procedure.
13. It records options, constraints and tradeoffs that must be adapted to workload and organization.
14. Cloud/region, edition, scale, data sensitivity, availability/recovery objectives, connectivity, ownership, cost and unsupported assumptions.
15. Document account/region boundaries, identity/data protection, replication/failover and tests, telemetry/incident ownership, data movement and cost attribution.
